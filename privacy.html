Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy covers the Kingpin Racing race strategy planner ("the app"), an internal tool used by Kingpin Racing team members to plan and track endurance race stints. It is not a public consumer product — access is limited to invited team members.

What we collect

  • Account information — when you sign in with Google or via emailed sign-in link, we receive your email address and basic profile information (name, profile picture) from your authentication provider.
  • Race planning data — information team members enter into the app: car and driver rosters, stint schedules, timing, in-race notes, flags, and finish results.

How we use it

  • To identify who you are when you sign in, and to determine your role (race engineer or driver) within the app.
  • To operate the race planning features themselves — building stint schedules, tracking live changes during a race, and archiving results.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties beyond what's needed to run the app (see below).

Who has access

Race data is visible to other signed-in team members using the app, per the roles set by your race engineer. It is not publicly accessible.

Third-party services

  • Google — used solely for authentication (Sign in with Google).
  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider, used to store account and race data securely.

Data retention & deletion

Race data is retained to preserve historical results. If you'd like your account data removed, contact us using the details below and we'll take care of it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in any meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Questions?
Contact Kingpin Racing at the details listed on our home page.
